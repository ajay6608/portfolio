About

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate who likes turning language models into tools that people can actually use.

My two projects both use the Gemini API. One answers questions about a CSV file, and the other scores resumes. In both, I spend most of my effort on making the model's output safe and consistent, because a clever demo is not much use if it breaks or can't be trusted.
